## Review & approve

When a task passes its gates it is **held for your review** — nothing merges without you.

- The **Needs Review** badge + board column show what's waiting.
- From a **Stream** row (or the toast) act inline: **Approve**, **Open Diff**, or **Abort**.
- A failed task shows **Tıkandı**; hit **Retry** to re-run it after a fix.

That's the loop: create → watch → review → approve. You stay the director.
