## Watch it build — without drowning in logs

Open the **Command Center** (`Cmd/Ctrl+Alt+C`) for the board, and the **Events** panel for the
live **Stream**: a readable, time-stamped line per step —

- *Geliştiriyor · 4dk · 2 dosya* · *Test ediliyor* · *Onay bekliyor* · *Birleştirildi ✓*

Severity colored (yellow = needs you, green = done, blue = active). The status bar shows a one-line
**Now**. Click any Stream row to drill into the **full event JSON** when you want the detail.
